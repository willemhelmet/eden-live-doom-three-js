REACTOR BRAND ASSETS

The core logo is the preferred mark. Use the separate Chip and wordmark only
when the format benefits from a more flexible arrangement.

Included files
- logo/symbol-black.svg / symbol-white.svg: Chip symbol
- logo/symbol-dune.svg / symbol-dune-dark.svg: colored Chip variants
- logo/wordmark-black.svg / wordmark-white.svg: Reactor wordmark
- logo/wordmark-dune.svg / wordmark-dune-dark.svg: colored wordmark variants
- raster/symbol-wordmark-*.png: core logo lockup in all four colors
- raster/symbol-*.png: optimized Chip raster variants
- raster/wordmark-*.png: optimized wordmark raster variants
- colors.css / colors.json: core and companion color tokens

Usage
- Keep clear space equal to one Chip around every side of the logo.
- Keep the core logo at least 100 px wide on screen or 15 mm wide in print.
- Use black or white for maximum contrast. Dune and Dune Dark are supplied for
  brand-led applications where the background provides sufficient contrast.
- Do not rotate, outline, redraw, retype, or rearrange the supplied artwork.

Core colors
- Interstellar: #000000
- Dune: #C7C099
- Whiteout: #FFFFFF

Companion tones
- Dune Light / Dark: #FDF5C6 / #555240
- Urban: #BDBDBD
